## 👨‍💻 About Me
I am a Master's student in **Data Science & Artificial Intelligence** at the University of Trieste, where my thesis focuses on combining **Large Language Models (LLMs)** with **genetic algorithms** to optimize code.  

In parallel with my studies, I work in industry, designing and implementing **backend applications** for enterprise use. My work involves leveraging **Azure cloud services** and integrating **LLMs** to boost productivity and streamline workflows.
